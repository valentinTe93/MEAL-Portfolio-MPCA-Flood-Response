# Performance Measurement Plan (PMP) / MEAL Plan

## Project

**Simulated Multipurpose Cash Assistance (MPCA) Flood Response**

This Performance Measurement Plan (PMP) was developed for a simulated humanitarian response to severe flooding affecting vulnerable households in coastal Côte d'Ivoire.

The PMP translates the project's Logframe results and indicators into a practical system for collecting, analyzing and using MEAL information.

The plan follows the **MEAL DPro Guide** as the primary methodological reference.

## Purpose

The PMP defines:

- What results will be monitored
- Which indicators will be used
- How data will be collected
- When data will be collected
- Who will collect the data
- Who or what will provide the information
- How the data will be analyzed
- Which subgroups may be compared
- How information will support communication and decision-making

It therefore provides the bridge between the project's results framework and the actual data-collection tools.

## Key Indicators

### Strategic Objective 1

**Households have increased economic capacity to access essential food and basic goods.**

Indicator:

**% of surveyed flood-affected households whose monthly per-capita economic capacity is at or above the applicable MEB threshold**

The indicator uses an ECMEN-based expenditure and resource module.

The analysis converts relevant expenditure and resource information into a monthly household economic capacity and then calculates the per-capita value.

The result is compared with the applicable contextual MEB/SMEB threshold.

The MEB threshold is not invented within the portfolio and must be defined using the appropriate contextual programme reference.

### Strategic Objective 2

**Households have improved food security and are better able to meet their food needs during the flood season.**

Indicator:

**% of surveyed flood-affected households with acceptable Food Consumption Score (FCS)**

The FCS uses the standard seven-day food-consumption recall and scoring methodology.

Indicator:

**Mean rCSI score among surveyed flood-affected households**

The Reduced Coping Strategies Index measures the frequency with which households use selected food-related coping strategies during the previous seven days.

Lower rCSI values generally indicate less reliance on these food-related coping strategies.

### Intermediate Result 1

**Eligible flood-affected households receive the planned cash assistance in full and on time.**

Indicators include:

- % of eligible households receiving the planned cash transfer in full
- % of eligible households receiving the planned transfer within the programme-defined delivery timeframe

These indicators use payment and transaction records, supported where necessary by household verification.

### Intermediate Result 2

**Assisted households are able to access the cash transfer through a functional transfer mechanism.**

Indicators include:

- % of assisted households successfully accessing cash
- % of assisted households reporting problems accessing cash

These indicators are collected through household monitoring/PDM after transfer cycles.

### Output 1

**Eligible flood-affected households are identified and verified for MPCA assistance.**

Indicator:

**% of targeted households successfully identified and verified as eligible according to programme criteria**

### Output 2

**Verified eligible households are processed for cash assistance through the selected transfer mechanism.**

Indicator:

**% of planned cash transfers successfully processed through the selected transfer mechanism**

### Output 3

**Households receive information on how to access and use the cash transfer and how to provide feedback or complaints.**

Indicator:

**% of assisted households reporting receipt of information on transfer access/use and available feedback/complaints mechanisms**

## Data Collection

The PMP combines several data sources and methods.

### Household Surveys

Used for:

- FCS
- rCSI
- ECMEN-based economic capacity
- Selected household monitoring questions

### Payment and Transaction Records

Used to monitor:

- Transfers received
- Transfer amounts
- Timeliness
- Successful and failed transactions
- Partial or discrepant payments

### Post-Distribution / Household Monitoring

Used to assess:

- Cash access
- Access problems
- Information received
- Feedback and complaints mechanisms
- Selected household-level experiences following assistance

### Market Monitoring

Market and contextual conditions are monitored because they are important assumptions in the Theory of Change.

Relevant information includes:

- Market accessibility
- Availability of essential goods
- Price conditions

## Baseline and Follow-Up

For the household-level outcome indicators, the project uses:

**Baseline → Follow-up**

The same core methodologies are intended to be maintained across measurement rounds to support meaningful comparison.

The portfolio baseline dataset is simulated and fictional.

It is not statistically representative of a real population.

## Data Analysis

Analysis includes:

- Percentages
- Means
- Indicator disaggregation
- Baseline/follow-up comparison
- Classification of FCS results
- rCSI scoring
- ECMEN-based calculations
- Identification of weaker locations or relevant household groups
- Review of programme delivery performance

The PMP emphasizes interpretation rather than simply producing numbers.

For example, a change in food security indicators should be interpreted alongside economic capacity, market conditions, cash access and other relevant contextual information.

## Data Quality

Data quality is addressed through:

- KoboToolbox validation rules
- Skip logic
- Conditional questions
- Required fields
- Calculation checks
- Dataset validation
- Independent recalculation of derived indicators
- Review of missing or inconsistent values
- Controlled baseline and follow-up methodologies

The clean simulated baseline dataset was independently checked before being locked for analysis.

## Data Use

The PMP is designed so that MEAL information can support decisions.

Potential uses include:

- Identifying transfer-delivery problems
- Addressing payment delays or discrepancies
- Identifying barriers to cash access
- Improving communication and feedback mechanisms
- Identifying weaker food-consumption outcomes
- Monitoring household economic capacity
- Understanding contextual market constraints
- Supporting programme adaptation

The objective is not simply to collect data, but to generate information that can be interpreted and used.

## Key Learning

Developing the PMP highlighted that choosing an indicator is only one part of measurement design.

For every indicator, it is necessary to think about:

**What exactly are we measuring?**

**Where will the data come from?**

**Who will collect it?**

**When will it be collected?**

**How will it be calculated?**

**How will it be analyzed?**

**What decision could the information support?**

This helped connect the conceptual results logic with the practical realities of data collection and analysis.

## Portfolio Note

This is a **simulated humanitarian MEAL project created for learning and professional skills demonstration**.

The programme scenario, household data and subsequent analysis are fictional and should not be interpreted as real humanitarian field evidence or programme results.
